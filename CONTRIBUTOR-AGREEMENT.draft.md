# Church CTV contributor agreement

Version 0.1 — REVIEW DRAFT — NOT YET ACCEPTED OR OPERATIVE

Purpose: preserve contributor ownership while allowing a shared church edition
and Licensor-operated commercial services. This is a separate proposed agreement,
not a claim that existing contributors already agreed to these terms.

Before accepting contributions, name the actual legal recipient and have counsel
approve this agreement and an auditable acceptance process. An organization
handle is not a substitute for the legal recipient. No blanket checkbox silently
changes a prior contribution's license.

Proposed terms:

1. You retain ownership of your contribution. You identify the submitted work and
   the account or entity authorizing its submission.
2. You grant the named Recipient a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive,
   royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, modify, prepare
   derivative works, publicly display/perform, distribute and sublicense your
   contribution, including under the finalized church-source license, other
   licenses and commercial service or distribution arrangements. This expressly
   includes commercial relicensing; it is not limited to the public church grant.
3. You grant a corresponding patent license, limited to claims you can license
   that are necessarily infringed by your contribution alone or its intended
   combination with the submitted project. Counsel must finalize defensive
   termination and the exact scope.
4. You represent that you have authority to make these grants, that required
   employer or co-owner approvals were obtained, and that you identify any
   third-party code or assets and their licenses. You do not submit secrets,
   personal information, customer content or materials you cannot license.
5. You provide the contribution as is, without an obligation to provide support
   or future work. Recipient need not accept, merge, publish or maintain it.
6. Recipient preserves legally required attribution. This agreement grants no
   trademark rights and does not authorize commercial use of other project code.
7. No payment, employment relationship, endorsement, copyright assignment or
   exclusive ownership results merely from a contribution.

Acceptance requirements before enabling outside contributions:

- Individual versus employer/corporate authority is recorded.
- Accepted agreement version, contributor identity, timestamp and covered
  contribution are auditable without publishing private legal records.
- A privileged maintainer verifies acceptance before merge. An untrusted pull
  request cannot self-certify the gate with an editable file or label.
- Existing third-party rights and incompatible contributions are reviewed
  independently; this agreement cannot create rights a contributor lacks.
- Privacy, governing law, withdrawal treatment, legal entity and archival process
  are finalized by counsel.

Until that process exists, do not merge external contributions into a release
that will be commercially operated or relicensed in reliance on this draft.
