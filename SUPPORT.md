# Permissions, sales and support

## Two channels, and which one to use

**Questions about the software: open an issue in this repository.** Anyone with a
GitHub account may open one, with no approval and no fee. Use the issue template
that fits; if none fits, open a blank issue. Questions are answered as time
allows, and an answer is advice from whoever answers, not a support commitment.
Tell us the repository, the version or commit, the platform and version
(Roku/Android/webOS/Tizen/Vizio), and what you expected versus what happened;
synthetic feed/config attachments are welcome.

**Permission, commercial-licensing, sales and support requests: email
`dominick253@gmail.com`.** That includes: church-eligibility confirmation under
license section 2.3, self-hosting or managed-hosting quotes, a written permission
that the license does not already grant, and anything involving a name, a
customer, a contract or money.

**Security problems: never a public issue.** Use the private channel in
`SECURITY.md`.

## What the project owner decides

The project owner and decision-maker is GitHub `@dominick253`. Ownership and
permission decisions, commercial licensing and any exception to the license are
the owner's alone; another maintainer, a contributor or an automated agent cannot
grant them on the owner's behalf, and opening an issue or emailing does not by
itself grant a right.

## What is public once you post it

Issues are public. Do not post credentials, API tokens, private keys, signing
material, member or viewer data, personal information, an unpublished
vulnerability, private endpoints, production configuration, customer names
attached to a commercial fact, or contractual terms. Do not email passwords,
signing keys or other secrets either; describe the credential and rotate it at
its issuer.

## What is not promised

The email address was supplied by the owner on 2026-11-10. No response time, no
support SLA, no uptime commitment, no hosted service, no store approval and no
obligation to answer an issue are represented, and none is created by using
these channels (license section 10). Existing third-party rights are unchanged.
