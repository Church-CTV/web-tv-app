# Generated example assets

The launcher PNGs and pinned splash were produced from the geometric pixel
functions in `scripts/gen-icons.mjs` using Node 24. No customer logo, external
image, font, screenshot or third-party trademark was used as an input.
Legacy `roku` basenames exist only to preserve current platform metadata paths.
The images are examples, not registered store branding or a Roku endorsement.

`scripts/tests/launcher-provenance.test.mjs` pins launcher byte hashes and sizes.
`scripts/tests/pinned-assets.test.mjs` compares splash pixels with the generator.
All rights in the generator still depend on the project's code-ownership review;
this provenance record is not a legal clearance certificate.
